Research

Patient-specific respiratory biofluid dynamics

Using CT-scan-reconstructed nasal, oral, and bronchial airway geometries, I simulate airflow, heat and moisture exchange, and aerosol and nanoparticle transport under physiological breathing conditions. This work combines polyhedral meshing, species-transport modelling, Lagrangian–Eulerian particle tracking, and custom boundary conditions implemented through user-defined functions, with systematic validation against published experimental data. Applications include inhalation therapy, environmental exposure assessment, and airway physiology.

CFD–experiment coupled studies of engineering systems

At IIT Bombay, I work on CFD analysis of domestic pressure regulators for LPG systems and hydrogen–air premixed micro-combustors, paired with experimental prototype testing for validation. Earlier, at CSIR-CMERI and IIT Jodhpur, I worked on hydrogen mixing and combustion in SI engines, bio-fuel combustion, and designed and fabricated a micro gas turbine test rig with full optical and emissions instrumentation.

Current interests

Interface-resolved two-phase simulation with the Basilisk solver (volume-of-fluid methods), droplet dynamics, indicator transport in cardiovascular flows, and mixing physics under pulsatile conditions.

Toolbox: ANSYS Fluent (incl. UDFs in C) · CONVERGE CFD · OpenFOAM · Basilisk · SolidWorks · MATLAB · Linux · Experimental instrumentation — high-speed & thermal imaging, spectrometry, DAQ, flow metering, gas analysis
